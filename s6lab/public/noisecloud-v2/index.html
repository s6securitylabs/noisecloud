A next-generation defensive media-carrier lab built from the S6 fork of NoiseCloud. V2 adds an S6NC1 envelope, SHA-256 verification, optional passphrase encryption, and slideshow-sideband video mode. The payload is still in video frames, not audio.

What changed from v1?

  • Original lab: raw upstream NoiseCloud MP4 carrier.
  • V2: payload is wrapped in S6NC1 before encoding, with filename, MIME, size, SHA-256, and optional AES-GCM encryption.
  • Slideshow sideband mode creates a visible slideshow/test-pattern video and preserves the carrier in a right-side video panel for reliable decoding.
  • Fork: github.com/s6securitylabs/noisecloud. Upstream remains github.com/unlucas-br/noisecloud.
